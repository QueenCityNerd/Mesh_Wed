How to Check In

Participating in the weekly STMesh net is simple! Open your Meshtastic app, change to the LongFast channel, and broadcast a message containing the tag #mwm along with your comments for the net.

Your check-in will automatically log to this roster with your timestamp, node short name, ID, and comment. Page auto-refreshes every 30 seconds.
